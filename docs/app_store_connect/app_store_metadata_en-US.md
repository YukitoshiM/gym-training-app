## Description

BodyMode is a health and fitness app that makes the answer to “What should I do today?” easier to see.

Choose from 12 coaches with different specialties and personalities. BodyMode uses the information you choose to record, such as weight, meals, sleep, readiness, activity, and workout history, to organize your readiness and up to three priority actions for today. Detailed records and analysis stay available when you need them.

KEY FEATURES

• Today's readiness and up to three priority actions
• 12 AI coaches with distinct goals, expertise, and communication styles
• Weight, waist, body-fat estimate, and body-measurement charts
• AI-assisted meal photo estimates with editable calories and macros
• Multi-angle progress-photo sets with AI reference analysis
• Workout plans built from a broad exercise library
• Set-by-set weight, reps, RPE, tempo, and rest tracking
• Plan-versus-result comparisons, PRs, and training volume
• Condition views combining sleep, activity, recovery, and sensor data
• Apple Watch menu selection, set controls, tempo haptics, and rest timers
• Optional Apple Health integration
• Light and dark appearance with two color themes
• JSON export and on-device data deletion

No account is required. Records are stored primarily on your device, and all manual logging features remain available without Apple Health or AI.

AI features require a network connection. You control which record categories may be shared with AI, and photo analysis starts only when you explicitly request it. If AI is unavailable, manual records, history, workouts, and Apple Watch features remain available. AI estimates and recommendations are reference information and may be reviewed or corrected.

BodyMode is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or determine disease. Consult a qualified healthcare professional if you have health concerns.
