# BodyMode Beta Social Caption

I started building BodyMode because I wanted a fitness app that I would actually find useful myself.

Recording workouts was not enough. I still had to decide what to train, whether to push harder or recover, and how to adjust when my condition changed. I wanted an AI trainer that could look at my training, recovery, meals, and progress together, then give me a clear plan for today.

I also wanted to take that plan into the gym and follow it easily from my iPhone or Apple Watch, without stopping to think about every set.

BodyMode is still in beta, so some parts may be unfinished or rough around the edges. But it is now ready enough that I would like to share it with friends and anyone interested, and see how it works for them.

If this sounds useful, please try it through TestFlight. I would really appreciate honest feedback about what works, what is confusing, and what you wish it could do.

https://testflight.apple.com/join/ApPJPygJ
