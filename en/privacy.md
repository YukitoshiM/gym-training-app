# BodyMode Privacy Policy

[日本語]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }})

Effective date: August 10, 2026  
Last updated: August 23, 2026

BodyMode is designed to help users remain in control of their health and training information. This policy explains where data is stored and how it is used.

## 1. Information We Handle

When you enter information or grant permission, BodyMode may handle your profile, goals, body measurements, meals, progress photos, workouts, sleep, activity, condition, gym visits, and settings. When you use AI, Sign in with Apple creates a BodyMode account. The server stores a pseudonymous ID derived from Apple's identifier, AI credit balance, grant and usage ledger, and purchase transaction IDs. Your name and Apple ID email address are not stored for AI credit management.

## 2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to save and display records, show progress toward goals, create charts and reports, sync with Apple Watch, perform AI analyses you select, improve quality, and investigate issues. Health information is not used to select ads.

## 3. On-Device Storage

BodyMode records, settings, the barcode product dictionary, and patterns such as completed or skipped daily recommendations are generally stored on your iPhone or Apple Watch. You can pause or reset the use of behavioral patterns in Settings. Record files on iPhone use file protection tied to the device lock. BodyMode's record folder is excluded from device backups. Export any records you need to retain from Settings.

## 4. Apple Health

With your permission, BodyMode may read or write steps, activity, sleep, heart rate, energy burned, workouts, body measurements, and related data. HealthKit data is not used for advertising, data sales, or credit decisions. You can change permissions in iPhone Settings or the Health app.

## 5. Photos

Meal and progress photos are used only when you select them. They are used to display your records and for AI analysis that you explicitly start. BodyMode does not claim to determine your health status or exact body-fat percentage from photos alone.

## 6. Location and Motion

When you enable the relevant features, location may be used to log gym visits, and Apple Watch motion data may be used to estimate repetitions and tempo. This information is not used to select ads.

## 7. AI Server

BodyMode sends selected data only when you start image analysis, a weekly or monthly report, a chat, or a scheduled report that you explicitly enabled. Chat requests may include your message, recent conversation, summaries from record categories you allowed, and memories you approved. Monthly reviews and proposed goals are drafts and are saved on your device only after you review or edit them. Memory candidates are never saved automatically.

Production AI requests pass through Cloudflare Workers to the OpenAI API. BodyMode sets `store: false` on OpenAI Responses API requests and does not store prompts, AI responses, photos, or body and health values in its D1 database or operational logs. OpenAI does not use API inputs or outputs to train its models by default. OpenAI may retain abuse-monitoring logs that can contain inputs, outputs, and images for up to 30 days, or longer where legally required. BodyMode does not opt in to sharing API data for training.

## 8. Diagnostic Information

BodyMode may store operational logs and diagnostic information supplied by Apple's MetricKit on your device. On Apple Watch, workout, set, and rest-timer actions; HealthKit status; iPhone communication results; and recorded weight, repetitions, or RPE may be held temporarily for up to 14 days or 500 entries and transferred to the paired iPhone through WatchConnectivity. iPhone diagnostic logs are retained until the first of 14 days, 1,000 entries, or 2 MiB is reached. Diagnostic logs are not sent automatically to the operator or a third party. You can delete them in Settings. They are used for issue investigation only if you export and attach them to a support request.

For authentication, credit management, and issue investigation, BodyMode's server stores a pseudonymous account ID, random request ID, feature, model, processing result, token counts, duration, and error category. It does not store conversation text, photos, body or health values, API keys, or access tokens. Non-reversible fingerprints derived from network or installation data are used for short-lived rate limiting and deleted after expiry. Account deletion removes the user's active database records, and pre-deletion data in daily backups expires within 14 days.

## 9. Usage Analytics

Usage analytics is off by default. If you enable it, BodyMode may send fixed events for screen choices, saved records, daily recommendations, notification opens, AI registration, insufficient-credit displays, credit screens, rewarded-ad outcomes, and purchase outcomes, together with time, app version, language, and distribution channel. They are associated with a pseudonymous installation identifier that is not tied to your name or email and are retained for up to 90 days. Analytics does not include purchase amount; body values such as weight; meal values or content; photos; exercise names, weight, or repetitions; heart-rate values; sleep duration; location; free-text content; AI messages; your name or email; or an advertising ID. From Settings, you can export on-device analytics, delete on-device and server-held analytics, or disable analytics.

## 10. Advertising and Tracking

The iPhone app uses Google AdMob for banner ads and rewarded video ads only when you choose to watch one. Ads are requested as non-personalized, and BodyMode does not request permission for cross-app tracking. Google may receive or generate your IP address, approximate location inferred from it, basic device and app information, SDK diagnostics, and ad impression or interaction data. Ad completion information and a unique reward transaction ID are used to prevent duplicate grants and add AI credits. BodyMode does not use or send HealthKit data, body values, heart rate, sleep, meals, photos, workouts, goals, GPS data, or gym locations for ad selection or marketing.

## 11. Sharing with Third Parties

BodyMode does not sell user information, except that information may be disclosed when required by law. Information may be processed by Apple, Cloudflare, OpenAI, and Google AdMob only as needed for the features described here, subject to each provider's terms and privacy policy.

## 12. Retention, Deletion, and Export

On-device records remain until you delete an item, delete all data, or remove the app. AI credit balances and the ledger remain on the server until account deletion and are not removed merely by uninstalling the app. Signing in again with the same Apple account restores access to the balance. Delete All Data in Settings also deletes the BodyMode account; unused purchased and promotional credits cannot be restored. Pseudonymous billing records required to prevent duplicate signup grants and reconcile App Store refunds are retained only as long as required for legal compliance and fraud prevention. AI trainer conversations remain on-device and can be exported or deleted separately. AI inputs and outputs that may be present in OpenAI abuse-monitoring logs are normally deleted within 30 days. Information written to Apple Health must also be managed in the Health app.

## 13. Security

BodyMode limits unnecessary external transmission, requests permissions by feature, and lets you choose which categories may be sent to AI. You are responsible for securing your device passcode, Apple ID, and any AI server credentials you configure.

## 14. Children

Use BodyMode with guidance from a parent, guardian, or qualified professional where appropriate for the user's age or health. BodyMode does not perform advertising profiling directed at children.

## 15. Changes and Contact

This policy may be updated as features or data practices change. Material changes will be announced in the app or on the public website. Contact [BodyMode Support](https://yukitoshim.github.io/gym-training-app/en/support/) with questions.
