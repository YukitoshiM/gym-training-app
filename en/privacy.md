# BodyMode Privacy Policy

[日本語]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }})

Effective date: August 10, 2026  
Last updated: August 15, 2026

BodyMode is designed to help users remain in control of their health and training information. This policy explains where data is stored and how it is used.

## 1. Information We Handle

When you enter information or grant permission, BodyMode may handle your profile, goals, body measurements, meals, progress photos, workouts, sleep, activity, condition, gym visits, and settings. For meals, BodyMode may store scanned product codes, product names, nutrition reference amounts, and nutrient values in an on-device product dictionary. The initial release does not create an in-app account.

## 2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to save and display records, show progress toward goals, create charts and reports, sync with Apple Watch, perform AI analyses you select, improve quality, and investigate issues. Health information is not used to select ads.

## 3. On-Device Storage

BodyMode records, settings, the barcode product dictionary, and patterns such as completed or skipped daily recommendations are generally stored on your iPhone or Apple Watch. You can pause or reset the use of behavioral patterns in Settings. Record files on iPhone use file protection tied to the device lock. BodyMode's record folder is excluded from device backups. Export any records you need to retain from Settings.

## 4. Apple Health

With your permission, BodyMode may read or write steps, activity, sleep, heart rate, energy burned, workouts, body measurements, and related data. HealthKit data is not used for advertising, data sales, or credit decisions. You can change permissions in iPhone Settings or the Health app.

## 5. Photos

Meal and progress photos are used only when you select them. They are used to display your records and for AI analysis that you explicitly start. BodyMode does not claim to determine your health status or exact body-fat percentage from photos alone.

## 6. Location and Motion

When you enable the relevant features, location may be used to log gym visits, and Apple Watch motion data may be used to estimate repetitions and tempo. This information is not used to select ads.

## 7. AI Server

Only when you enable an AI feature and start image analysis, a weekly or monthly report, or a chat does BodyMode send selected data to the AI server configured for the distributed app. Chat requests may include your message, recent conversation, summaries from record categories you allowed, and memories you approved. Monthly reviews and proposed goals are shown as drafts and are saved on your device only after you review or edit them. Memory candidates are never saved automatically; only the items you select are stored on your device. The AI trainer backend currently provided by BodyMode is stateless and does not save conversations, context, or memories to a database or file. If you change the connection destination, that server operator's terms govern its storage and processing.

## 8. Diagnostic Information

BodyMode may store operational logs and diagnostic information supplied by Apple's MetricKit on your device. On Apple Watch, workout, set, and rest-timer actions; HealthKit status; iPhone communication results; and recorded weight, repetitions, or RPE may be held temporarily for up to 14 days or 500 entries and transferred to the paired iPhone through WatchConnectivity. iPhone diagnostic logs are retained until the first of 14 days, 1,000 entries, or 2 MiB is reached. Diagnostic logs are not sent automatically to the operator or a third party. You can delete them in Settings. They are used for issue investigation only if you export and attach them to a support request.

For authentication and issue investigation, the AI server records processing outcome and duration, along with non-reversible HMAC fingerprints of the IP address, installation ID, and app version. Conversation text, photos, body or health values, API keys, and access tokens are not written to logs. Logs rotate at 5 MiB or 14 days, and one backup generation is retained for no more than 14 days.

## 9. Usage Analytics

Usage analytics is off by default. If you enable it, BodyMode may send predefined events such as screen selections, saved records, daily recommendation views, starts, changes, completion categories, and opened notifications to the service operator. Events may also include fixed categories for goal, experience, today's readiness, recommendation category, display position, recommendation source, completion method, and replacement reason, together with time, app version, and language. They are associated with a pseudonymous installation identifier that is not tied to your name or email and are retained for up to 90 days. Analytics does not include body values such as weight; meal values or content; photos; exercise names, weight, or repetitions; heart-rate values; sleep duration; location; free-text content; AI messages; your name or email; or an advertising ID. From Settings, you can export on-device analytics, delete on-device and server-held analytics, or disable analytics.

## 10. Advertising and Tracking

The iPhone app displays banner ads using the Google AdMob SDK. Ads are requested as non-personalized, and BodyMode does not request permission for cross-app tracking. For ad delivery, Google may receive or generate your IP address, approximate location inferred from the IP address, basic device and app information, SDK crash and performance data, and ad impression or interaction data. BodyMode does not use or send HealthKit data, body values, heart rate, sleep, meals, photos, workouts, goals, GPS data collected by BodyMode, or gym locations for ad selection or marketing. Google's consent message is shown where required and can be reopened from Settings. See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for Google's practices.

## 11. Sharing with Third Parties

BodyMode does not sell user information, except that information may be disclosed when required by law. Information may leave the device only as needed for the features described here, including an AI connection, Apple operating-system services, a share action you initiate, and ad delivery by Google AdMob.

## 12. Retention, Deletion, and Export

Records and the barcode product dictionary remain on your device until you delete an item, delete all app data, or remove the app. AI trainer conversations are limited to 200 entries and approved memories to 100 entries on the device; they can be removed from their respective screens or by deleting all data. Diagnostic logs and usage analytics are subject to the limits described above. Settings lets you export all records, including the product dictionary, as JSON and delete all app data or diagnostic logs. Information written to Apple Health must also be managed in the Health app.

## 13. Security

BodyMode limits unnecessary external transmission, requests permissions by feature, and lets you choose which categories may be sent to AI. You are responsible for securing your device passcode, Apple ID, and any AI server credentials you configure.

## 14. Children

Use BodyMode with guidance from a parent, guardian, or qualified professional where appropriate for the user's age or health. BodyMode does not perform advertising profiling directed at children.

## 15. Changes and Contact

This policy may be updated as features or data practices change. Material changes will be announced in the app or on the public website. Contact [BodyMode Support]({{ '/en/support/' | relative_url }}) with questions.
